Terms of use

The agreement between you and Heron, in plain English.

Heron can make mistakes

Heron uses AI to read your school emails. It can miss things, or get them wrong: a date, who something is for, or what you need to do. Your school’s own messages are always the record. Heron is there to help you keep on top of them, not to replace reading them. Every task in Heron leads back to the message it came from, so you can check.

Who can use Heron

Heron is for parents and carers of children at the schools it supports, in the UK. While it’s tested with a few families, it’s by invitation only. You must be 18 or over.

Your account

  • You sign in with a code sent to your email address. Keep access to that email to yourself.
  • You can invite another parent or carer to share your family’s Heron. They’ll see everything your family does.
  • Only forward emails you’re entitled to: the ones the school sends you about your own children.

The test

During the test, Heron is free. It may change, and things may not always work. You’ll be told before Heron starts to cost anything, and nothing is ever charged without you choosing to subscribe.

What Heron is responsible for

Heron will take reasonable care to run reliably and to keep your data safe. Because Heron can make mistakes, and your school’s messages are the record, Heron isn’t responsible for anything missed or misunderstood because of what it showed or didn’t show you. Nothing in these terms limits your rights under consumer law, or Heron’s responsibility for anything the law doesn’t allow it to exclude.

Ending

You can stop using Heron and delete your account at any time, in Settings. Heron may close an account that’s misused, after telling you why, where it can.

Changes

If these terms change, Heron will ask you to agree again before you carry on.
